# Habit reminders

A reminder tells you at a set time that a habit is not done yet. It shows once a day, only on days the habit is scheduled, and only while the goal is not met.

## Set a reminder

1. Choose **New habit**, or select a habit and choose **Edit habit**. See [Habits](habits.md#create-a-habit).
2. Under **Reminder**, turn on **Remind me** and set the time. The time starts at 20:00.
3. Choose **Create habit** or **Save**.

The first time you save a habit with a reminder, Logwell asks for permission to show system notifications. To stop a reminder, turn off **Remind me** and save.

A reminder that you add, or move to a time that has already passed today, starts tomorrow. Moving the time later today makes it remind you again today.

## Act on a reminder

The reminder is a notice, such as "Time for Read · 20 of 30 min". It stays until you act on it or close it.

- **Check in** checks a done-or-not habit in and adds 1 to a count. For an amount, it opens the Habits page with the amount field ready. The notice that follows offers **Undo**.
- **Snooze 10 min** reminds you again after ten minutes if the goal is still not met. Checking in during the snooze cancels it.
- Closing the notice ends the reminder for today.

When several habits are due at once, one notice names them all, with **Open habits**.

While Obsidian is in the background, a system notification appears too, if you allowed it. Click it to open the Habits page.

## Know when reminders run

- Reminders run only while Obsidian is open. If Obsidian is closed at the reminder time, the reminder appears when you open it later that day.
- For a goal per week, a reminder shows only while the week's goal is not met and you have no check-in today.
- An archived habit never reminds you.
- Each device where Obsidian is open reminds you on its own, so a reminder can appear on more than one device. A check-in on another device stops it here only after your database syncs.
- The time follows the **Time zone** setting. See [Settings and commands](settings-and-commands.md#time).

If a reminder did not appear, see [A habit reminder did not fire](troubleshooting.md#a-habit-reminder-did-not-fire).
